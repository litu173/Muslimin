Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 (v1.5)

Muslimin ("the app") helps Muslims find jamat times of nearby masjids. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account: name and email address, and an encrypted password if you sign up with email (or your Google sign-in), handled by Google Firebase Authentication. An account is needed to use the app.
  • Followed masjids and reminder settings — stored on your phone and in your account, so they sync between devices. App preferences such as language and light/dark theme stay on your phone.
  • Location — used on your device to find nearby masjids and calculate prayer times. We do not store your location history.
  • Notification token — to send notices from masjids you follow.
  • Masjid authorities only: phone number, NID number and role, used only to verify the masjid and never shown publicly; and the masjid's location (with its GPS accuracy), which is shown on the public masjid profile and can be updated by the masjid's owner.

How we use it

Only to run the app: show masjids, send reminders and notices, sync your settings and verify masjid profiles. We do not sell your data or show ads.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, asia-south1). Access is protected by security rules: only you can read your account data; NID and phone numbers of masjid authorities are visible only to them and to Muslimin administrators.

Your choices

  • Change your name or password, or reset a forgotten password, from the app.
  • Delete your account any time: More → My Account → Delete Account. This removes your account and saved data.
  • Turn off location or notifications in your phone settings (some features need them).

Contact

Questions or data requests? Open an issue at github.com/litu173/Muslimin/issues.
